YOPO Autonomous Navigation Demo

Five YOPO autonomous flight recordings across real cities — Hong Kong, Los Angeles, New York and Sydney: continuous 3D avoidance, building weaving, self-escape when boxed in, and long-distance autonomous navigation.

Continuous avoidance (Hong Kong)

Inside real Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles city blocks, YOPO's end-to-end network performs continuous 3D obstacle avoidance while weaving through buildings.

Escape (Hong Kong)

When trapped by dense obstacles, YOPO autonomously finds a safe path and escapes smoothly.

Long flight (Los Angeles)

A long-distance autonomous city flight under YOPO navigation, showing sustained avoidance and trajectory tracking.

Long flight (New York)

A long-distance autonomous flight over New York, in the same autonomous-navigation and avoidance style.

Long flight (Sydney)

A long-distance autonomous flight over Sydney, validating cross-city generalization. Note: buildings with gaps beneath them (including vertical mesh structures, as shown in the video) cannot be passed underneath — the map is built from satellite scans taken from above, so both the buildings and the empty space below carry collision bodies, yet the avoidance rays do not react to such "empty" colliders.
