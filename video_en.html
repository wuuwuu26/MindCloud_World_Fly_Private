Wing-envelope guard

Reserves clearance for the airframe and wingspan in every direction, so the drone never grazes a wing when flying close to obstacles.

Horizontal detour

Raises the detour speed ceiling and radial push-off when blocked, making wall-hugging passes smoother and scrape-free.

End-to-end navigation

Once a goal is set, YOPO directly outputs control commands, avoiding obstacles in real 3D using DA360 depth estimates.
